# Business questions and intended decisions

## 01. How are revenue and enrolments trending against target?
**Why it matters:** Executive KPI cards + monthly trend; identify growth or target gaps.

## 02. Which courses contribute most to revenue and completion outcomes?
**Why it matters:** Course comparison; balance commercial performance with learning outcomes.

## 03. Which acquisition channels have the best lead-to-enrolment conversion?
**Why it matters:** Channel efficiency; shift spend toward channels with stronger conversion.

## 04. Which regions and courses have the weakest completion rates?
**Why it matters:** Find where learner outcomes need intervention.

## 05. How much are we spending to acquire each enrolment?
**Why it matters:** Marketing cost per enrolment by channel and month.

## 06. Are refunds and support demand rising alongside enrolments?
**Why it matters:** Monitor service quality and potential friction.

## 07. Which teams are missing project delivery commitments?
**Why it matters:** Track completion rate and overdue projects by team.

## 08. Are operating teams staying within budget?
**Why it matters:** Compare actual cost with budget and investigate variance.
